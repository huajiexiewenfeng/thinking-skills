# Style 6 Cinematic Conceptual v3 Design

## Goal

Qualify `cinematic-conceptual` as a stable v3 article-visual style pack that turns one reflective thesis into a cinematic narrative landscape.

The pack must stabilize landscape depth, camera, journey line, color temperature, human scale, and title behavior without allowing a golden road, figure, destination, or story to authorize article-specific meaning.

## Selected Direction

Use a cinematic digital matte-painting landscape rather than an abstract editorial sculpture.

The direction is anchored by the user's earlier preferred result:

- a layered teal-blue valley or open terrain;
- one continuous road, river, trail, or ridge leading from foreground to horizon;
- one warm golden sunrise or sunset field;
- at most one small rear-view human figure anchoring scale and reflection;
- an active upper-left field reserved for deterministic title overlay;
- restrained painterly realism with emotional depth but no fantasy spectacle.

The first tactile-mineral gate candidate is retained only as a rejected audit sample. It was visually stable but removed the landscape, journey, horizon, and human narrative that define the intended mode.

## Visual Identity

- fully opaque landscape filling the frame;
- deep teal foreground based on `#173A43`;
- muted blue-gray distance layers based on `#4F6973`;
- one pale journey line based on `#B9C6C7`;
- one dominant warm golden horizon based on `#F2BC63`;
- restrained 35 mm elevated wide perspective with a stable horizon;
- four readable depth bands: foreground, midground, distant terrain, horizon;
- one winding journey line with no branches;
- at most one small rear-view figure occupying roughly eight to twelve percent of image height;
- optional sparse turquoise technology traces only when confirmed article semantics supports them;
- deterministic title panel and exact typography after image generation.

The style must not contain generated text, visible faces, multiple people, multiple roads, multiple suns, portals, cosmic scenery, neon circuitry, isometric infrastructure, infographic cards, or arbitrary data particles.

## Neighbor Boundaries

Style 6 differs from Style 3 by using natural terrain, one journey, one warm horizon, and only optional semantically justified technology traces instead of luminous circuitry and neon systems.

Style 6 differs from Style 5 by using elevated cinematic landscape perspective and human-scale narrative rather than fixed isometric modules and explicit infrastructure topology.

Style 6 differs from Style 1 by using painterly atmospheric depth and emotional journey rather than bright flat geometric explanation.

## Golden Roles

### Cover

Create a complete wide landscape in which one journey line enters from the foreground, crosses layered terrain, and leads toward one warm horizon. Use one small rear-view figure at the lower-right when the confirmed thesis supports reflection or continuation. Preserve an active upper-left title-safe region; generated imagery remains text-free.

### Concept

Use a different terrain family and relation from the cover. Explain one pause, choice, transition, threshold, or continuation through one landscape cue. Human presence is optional and never shows a face. The cover reference contributes only lens, palette, depth, atmospheric restraint, journey visibility, human scale, and finish.

### Simplified Landscape Map

Style 6 does not support dense technical diagrams. Render only a validated small graph as a human-free cinematic terrain map with two to five large landmarks and explicit route direction. Deterministic blank label zones are added without node-card grammar. The golden graph is non-authoritative for later articles.

## Reference Contract

Same-role goldens may contribute only camera family, landscape depth, palette, atmosphere, journey readability, human scale, title behavior, and painterly finish. They never authorize labels, numbers, nodes, topology, example story, road shape, person placement, destination, or article claims.

## Qualification

Qualification requires three user-approved opaque goldens, pinned v3 contracts, three unrelated compiling probes, explicit Style 3/5/1 discrimination, and release validation.

Reject any candidate that becomes a generic travel poster, stock lone-person scene, oversaturated sunrise, fantasy landscape, neon system, empty fog field, abstract sculpture, or generated-text cover.
