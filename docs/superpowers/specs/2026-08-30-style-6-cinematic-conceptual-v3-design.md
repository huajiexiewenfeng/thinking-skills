# Style 6 Cinematic Conceptual v3 Design

## Goal

Qualify `cinematic-conceptual` as a stable v3 article-visual style pack that turns one precise thesis into one memorable tactile editorial scene.

The pack must stabilize camera, material, atmosphere, light ratio, symbolic density, and title-space behavior without allowing a golden metaphor to authorize article-specific meaning.

## Selected Direction

Use a cinematic tactile editorial stage rather than generic science-fiction spectacle or a photoreal movie still.

The result should resemble a high-end magazine concept photograph built as a practical miniature set:

- one substantial sculptural metaphor;
- matte mineral, paper, or stone material;
- a deep desaturated blue environment;
- one practical amber light source or accent;
- layered but restrained atmospheric depth;
- enough environmental space for editorial hierarchy.

It must not resemble a glowing portal poster, fantasy landscape, stock lone-person scene, luxury-product advertisement, or neon systems visualization.

## Visual Identity

The required visual grammar is:

- fully opaque deep navy environment based on `#10151D`;
- desaturated blue-slate distance layers based on `#263445` and `#596C85`;
- one matte mineral subject based on `#D6DEE6`;
- exactly one warm amber thesis cue based on `#E59A3A`;
- fixed restrained 50 mm cinematic perspective with no fisheye or extreme wide-angle distortion;
- one cool ambient fill and one motivated warm practical light;
- subtle volumetric atmosphere only where the practical light passes through space;
- low film grain, broad tonal fields, strong silhouette, and very low symbolic density;
- clear foreground, midground, and background separation without unrelated scenery.

The style must not contain:

- multiple metaphors, multiple warm accents, rainbow grading, or decorative glow;
- default faces, anonymous lone people, crowds, hands, or human drama used as a shortcut;
- cosmic skies, floating orbs, fantasy cities, neon portals, holograms, or science-fiction interfaces;
- glossy product materials, chrome, transparent glass, luxury-advertising reflections, or oversharpened photoreal detail;
- infographic cards, node grids, dashboards, server architecture, isometric modules, or blueprint lines;
- generated title text, logos, labels, or article-specific claims.

## Neighbor Boundaries

### Style 3: Neon Systems

Style 6 uses tactile matte objects, one motivated amber practical light, restrained haze, and one symbolic thesis. Style 3 uses luminous circuitry, transparent or emissive structures, multiple system relationships, and neon technological atmosphere. Any hologram, circuit network, glass panel, electric cyan-magenta glow, or multi-node system should fail Style 6 qualification.

### Style 5: Isometric Infrastructure

Style 6 uses cinematic perspective, environmental depth, silhouette, and one metaphor. Style 5 uses fixed isometric projection, repeated infrastructure modules, explicit operational paths, and architectural-system readability. Any isometric module family, platform grid, service topology, or infrastructure diorama should fail Style 6 qualification.

### Style 1: Technical Editorial Minimal

Style 6 is atmosphere-led, tactile, and depth-driven. Style 1 is bright, flat, geometric, and explanatory. Adding a dark background and dramatic light to flat editorial geometry does not create Style 6.

## Golden Roles

### Cover

Create one complete cinematic environment around one monumental sculptural metaphor. The first golden uses a massive matte mineral gate whose narrow aperture admits one precise amber path into layered blue darkness. The subject occupies the right and central field while the left field remains a deterministic title-safe region.

The gate, aperture, and amber path form one metaphorical subject, not three independent symbols. No person, text, icon, machinery, or additional destination appears.

### Concept

Use a different object family and spatial relation from the cover. The first golden uses one large tilted matte slab held above a dark gap by one small amber wedge. The relation must read as “one precise support changes the state of one larger body” without promising strength, safety, or permanence.

The cover reference contributes only lens, palette, matte material, light ratio, atmospheric restraint, scale, and editorial finish. It does not authorize the gate, aperture, path, or topology.

### Simplified Metaphor Map

Style 6 does not support dense technical diagrams. The diagram role renders only a validated small graph as a cinematic environmental map with two to five large landmarks, one dominant route or threshold, deterministic blank label zones, and no node-card grammar.

Every landmark and route segment must correspond to the frozen graph. The golden map's example landmarks, positions, labels, edges, and story are non-authoritative. Human figures are forbidden.

## Reference Contract

Each same-role golden may contribute only observable style properties:

- restrained 50 mm cinematic perspective;
- practical-set scale and matte mineral material;
- deep navy environment and layered blue distance;
- one motivated amber practical light or thesis cue;
- silhouette strength, atmospheric restraint, crop behavior, and title-space behavior.

Every compiled prompt must forbid copying golden labels, numbers, nodes, topology, and example story.

During golden production, one approved earlier same-profile candidate may serve as a non-semantic bootstrap reference. It never authorizes a metaphor, object, claim, graph, light meaning, or article fact.

## Qualification Strategy

Qualification requires:

1. Three user-approved fully opaque golden assets: cover, concept, and simplified metaphor map.
2. A `visual-dna.json`, `role-contracts.json`, and `reference-matrix.json` pinned by SHA-256 in `golden-set.json`.
3. Successful compilation and linting for unrelated cover, concept, and diagram probes.
4. Explicit discrimination from Style 3 neon systems, Style 5 isometric infrastructure, and Style 1 technical editorial minimal.
5. Release validation with all golden hashes, approval state, and qualification fields passing.

## Failure Handling

Reject and revise any candidate that:

- looks like generic AI cinema, a glowing portal poster, a fantasy landscape, or a luxury advertisement;
- uses a face, lone person, crowd, or dramatic human silhouette as a shortcut;
- contains several symbols or scenery elements competing for meaning;
- has multiple warm accents, decorative glow, neon circuitry, glass, chrome, or rainbow grading;
- lacks a readable subject, has excessive empty darkness, or crops the metaphor ambiguously;
- invents article semantics, generated text, labels, logos, or topology;
- is transparent, crushed-black, muddy, excessively noisy, or illegible at article width.

User approval remains image-specific. Rejected images never become references, and the contract is not weakened merely to make a candidate pass.

## Scope

This phase changes only Style 6 contracts, goldens, evaluation records, regression probes, registry qualification, and the tests or documentation required to support them. It does not reopen Style 4, alter completed styles, change article semantics, or change publication Markdown formatting.
