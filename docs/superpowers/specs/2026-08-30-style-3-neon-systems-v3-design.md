# Style 3 Neon Systems v3 Design

## Decision

Style 3 becomes **deep editorial signal architecture**, not generic cyberpunk imagery.

The visual family is a fully opaque deep navy technical substrate containing three to five substantial extruded modules. A single cyan signal route creates the primary reading order. Acid green appears only for one confirmed active state or result. The camera is a restrained front-oblique cutaway with stable verticals, not a fixed isometric toy-world view and not flat blueprint drafting.

## Why the v2 profile drifts

The v2 protocol defines colors and glow limits, but it does not freeze physical scale, camera family, module mass, complete-frame occupancy, or the difference between semantic emission and decorative neon. Image models can therefore satisfy the words “dark modular system” while producing a cyberpunk city, HUD collage, glass maze, game poster, tiny object on black, or glow around every object.

## Visual thesis

One substantial dark system becomes understandable because one cyan route passes through it and activates exactly one acid-green state.

## Stable family grammar

- Fully opaque near-black navy ground with readable matte detail.
- Three to five large modules sharing one scale, bevel family, and shadow direction.
- Front-oblique cutaway perspective at roughly fifteen degrees of elevation.
- One continuous cyan focal route; subordinate cyan edges only when confirmed.
- Acid green only for one confirmed active state, never general decoration.
- Medium-low density and approximately 72–84% occupied frame.
- Text-free generation followed by deterministic title, labels, numbers, and legends.

## Role separation

### Cover

One complete system subject with one dominant signal route, one active state, and intentional dark architectural title space. It must read at thumbnail size without generated labels.

### Concept

One transformation, propagation, gate, or routing relation through two to four modules. It must not repeat the cover composition or invent an implementation.

### Diagram

A strict human-free frozen graph. Every module, port, edge, direction, group, exception, return, and active state must match validated semantics. Perspective and glow may never obscure topology.

## Three proposed golden images

1. **Cover — Signal Vault**: one complete dark signal architecture spanning the frame; a cyan route enters through three substantial chambers and activates one acid-green core on the right; upper-left remains an active title field.
2. **Concept — Controlled Handoff**: one cyan pulse passes from a broad intake module through one narrow transformation gate into one calm acid-green receiving chamber; no people, labels, code, or additional branch.
3. **Diagram — Verified Runtime Path**: a human-free five-node frozen graph on a dark technical substrate with cyan primary flow, one declared acid-green active output, and one restrained dashed exception/return route.

## Rejection boundaries

Reject any candidate that resembles a city, HUD, dashboard, hacker scene, gaming poster, server skyline, rainbow neon, magenta spectacle, glossy glass maze, isometric toy factory, transparent cutout, tiny isolated object, or topology hidden by bloom.

## Golden usage rule

Golden images authorize camera, substrate, physical scale, module material, route hierarchy, emission restraint, spacing, and finish only. Later article work must replace all labels, numbers, nodes, topology, and example story with validated article semantics.
