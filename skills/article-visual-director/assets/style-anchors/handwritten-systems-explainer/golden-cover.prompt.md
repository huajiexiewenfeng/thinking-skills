# Style 8 Golden Cover Prompt — Human-Free Revision

Use case: permanent Style 8 cover reference for article visual production.
Lesson objective: show that the same AI multiplier expands an existing capability gap: 10 × 100 = 1000, while 1 × 100 = 100.
Reference grammar: warm ivory paper; fine lively near-black handwritten ink; pastel-to-medium semantic fills; rounded formula boxes; direct arrows; dashed grouping boundaries; compact handwritten annotations; non-human annotation key; highlighted heading; irregular underline; high information density with disciplined whitespace. Black section tabs are allowed for group headings only. Preserve the shared material and layout grammar of the supplied references, not their original wording, technical subject, character identity, face, pose, silhouette, or people count.
Composition: 16:9 wide teaching whiteboard. A non-human annotation key at the left introduces two organized horizontal comparison lanes using capability bars, document stacks, and formula marks. Both lanes pass through visually identical orange AI multiplier boxes. The upper lane begins with a stronger blue starting structure and ends in a large coherent green output; the lower lane begins with a weaker blue structure and ends in a smaller green output. A restrained coral bracket on the right makes the widened absolute gap visible. Add two or three pastel annotation swatches and one dashed group boundary. Keep the central multiplier meaningful in a square crop.
Color behavior: warm paper #F7F2E7; ink #242321; semantic node fills may use medium blue #6CAFE0, orange #F2A13A, and green #65BD82; annotations use pastel blue #BFDDEE, mint #CFE8C7, sand #F3D39A, lavender #D9C8EA, and butter yellow #F4E59C; coral #DF6657 only for the gap or exception.
Line and material: roughly 2 px fine variable ink at 1600×900, slightly wobbly and hand-drawn, flat marker/watercolor fills with small registration offsets, subtle paper grain, no glossy depth.
Text plan: no generated readable Chinese title or paragraph text. Keep formula/annotation areas blank or use only simple non-critical diagram marks; exact Chinese and numbers will be rendered deterministically. No logo or watermark.
Avoid: no people, faces, hands, instructors, narrators, mascots, characters, anthropomorphic robots, or face-like AI icons; thick cartoon outlines, every-node black tabs, uncontrolled saturation, generic corporate flowchart cards, sparse decorative doodles, 3D/isometric objects, gradients, glassmorphism, photorealism, fake formulas, generated gibberish, UI chrome.
