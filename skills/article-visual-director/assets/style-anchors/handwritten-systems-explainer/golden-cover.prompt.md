# Style 8 Golden Cover Candidate Prompt — Reference-led Revision

Use case: WeChat article cover candidate and permanent Style 8 reference.
Lesson objective: show that the same AI multiplier expands an existing capability gap: 10 × 100 = 1000, while 1 × 100 = 100.
Reference grammar: warm ivory paper; fine lively near-black handwritten ink; pastel-to-medium semantic fills; rounded formula boxes; direct arrows; dashed grouping boundaries; compact handwritten annotations; one miniature instructor; highlighted heading; irregular underline; high information density with disciplined whitespace. Black section tabs are allowed for group headings only. Preserve the shared grammar of all four supplied references, not their original wording or technical subject.
Composition: 16:9 wide teaching whiteboard. One instructor at the left points toward two organized horizontal comparison lanes. Both lanes pass through visually identical orange AI multiplier boxes. The upper lane begins with a stronger blue starting structure and ends in a large coherent green output; the lower lane begins with a weaker blue structure and ends in a smaller green output. A restrained coral bracket on the right makes the widened absolute gap visible. Add two or three pastel annotation swatches and one dashed group boundary. Keep the central multiplier meaningful in a square crop.
Color behavior: warm paper #F7F2E7; ink #242321; semantic node fills may use medium blue #6CAFE0, orange #F2A13A, and green #65BD82; annotations use pastel blue #BFDDEE, mint #CFE8C7, sand #F3D39A, lavender #D9C8EA, and butter yellow #F4E59C; coral #DF6657 only for the gap or exception.
Line and material: roughly 2 px fine variable ink at 1600×900, slightly wobbly and human, flat marker/watercolor fills with small registration offsets, subtle paper grain, no glossy depth.
Text plan: no generated readable Chinese title or paragraph text. Keep formula/annotation areas blank or use only simple non-critical diagram marks; exact Chinese and numbers will be rendered deterministically. No logo or watermark.
Avoid: thick cartoon outlines, every-node black tabs, uncontrolled saturation, generic corporate flowchart cards, sparse decorative doodles, 3D/isometric objects, gradients, glassmorphism, photorealism, fake formulas, generated gibberish, UI chrome.
