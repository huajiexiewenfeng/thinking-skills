# Soft Technical Sketch

## Identity

- `profile_id`: `soft-technical-sketch`
- Protocol version: `2`
- 中文名: 柔和技术手绘
- Promise: teach one approachable mental model with fine pencil/ink and muted watercolor on warm paper.

## Use When

Use for tutorials, beginner-oriented explanations, learning journeys, and technical concepts that benefit from friendly physical metaphors.

## Do Not Use When

Do not use for forceful systems trade-offs, launch energy, corporate editorial layouts, or dense exact architecture.

## Mode Boundary

The nearest neighbor is Handwritten Systems Explainer. Style 7 uses fine soft 2 px contours, low-saturation watercolor, open captions, and gentle teaching objects; Style 8 uses bold variable 4 px black ink, saturated semantic fills, black label tabs, and direct system arrows.

## Required Visual Traits

- Warm paper, fine pencil/ink contours, muted blue/sage/ochre washes, friendly readable deterministic type, and low density.
- Simple teaching objects or spatial metaphors, generous margins, disciplined imperfection, and gentle front or shallow-oblique view.
- Color behaves like translucent watercolor support, never like solid high-saturation system coding.

## Allowed Variation

Objects may be notebooks, bridges, containers, paths, shelves, or tools. A small human figure is allowed only when it clarifies scale or learning action rather than becoming a mascot.

## Forbidden Traits

No childish doodles, mascots by default, corporate cards, bold black frames, black tabs, high saturation, thick direct arrows, faux notebook clutter, or inconsistent proportions.

## Cover Contract

Use one approachable teaching metaphor with ample warm-paper space and a deterministic title area. Keep contours fine and color washes muted even when the cover is more expressive.

## Concept Contract

Show one mental model through one to three familiar objects and a gentle relation. Prefer spatial explanation over boxes; avoid dense written notes.

## Deterministic Diagram Contract

Map paper to `surface.background`, contours/text to `semantic_color_roles.outline`, concept groups to `concept_a` and `concept_b`, the single emphasis to `emphasis`, captions to the `open-caption` style, and placement to the 24 px grid. Use 2 px slightly imperfect strokes, open labels, low-opacity washes, and no black tabs.

## Imagegen Prompt Contract

State the teaching objective, one familiar metaphor, simple low-density composition, fine pencil/ink contours, warm watercolor paper, muted blue/sage/ochre roles, generous margins, target aspect ratio, and text-free background. References anchor softness, wash transparency, object simplicity, and margin behavior. Negatives: bold black outlines, saturated fills, black tabs, corporate cards, childish doodles, labels, and dense architecture.

## Reference Use Contract

Use the cover reference for warmth and title-safe composition, concept reference for teaching metaphor and watercolor restraint, and diagram reference for fine contour, open captions, spacing, and exact muted tokens. Do not import decorative doodles.

## Validation Rubric

Pass only if the image feels calm and teachable, contours remain fine, saturation is low, captions are open, paper texture is subtle, objects keep consistent proportions, Style 8 black tabs/bold strokes are absent, and deterministic assets retain the same softness.
