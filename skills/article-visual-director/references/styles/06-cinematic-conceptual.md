# Cinematic Conceptual

## Identity

- `profile_id`: `cinematic-conceptual`
- Protocol version: `2`
- 中文名: 电影感概念视觉
- Promise: turn one precise thesis into one memorable symbolic subject with controlled atmosphere and light.

## Use When

Use for strategic or philosophical essays about control, uncertainty, trust, emergence, dependence, or change when a single metaphor can carry the argument.

## Do Not Use When

Do not use for multi-step tutorials, exact architecture, dense comparisons, or when no precise metaphor has been agreed.

## Mode Boundary

The nearest neighbor is Technical Editorial Minimal. Cinematic Conceptual is atmospheric, organic, and depth-led around one symbol; Style 1 is flat, geometric, grid-led, and explanatory. Adding dramatic light to a diagram does not make it Style 6.

## Required Visual Traits

- One symbolic subject, one controlled warm accent, strong silhouette, atmospheric depth, and a large environment with intentional negative space.
- Light supports the thesis and separates subject from environment; it does not decorate every surface.
- The image remains editorial and conceptually specific, with no default face or generated title text.

## Allowed Variation

The metaphor may be object, landscape, threshold, bridge, container, or controlled human silhouette. Camera distance and atmosphere may vary while one subject and one accent remain dominant.

## Forbidden Traits

No multi-module infographic, vague spectacle, default close-up face, dashboard geometry, multiple metaphors, rainbow grading, embedded title, or cinematic detail unrelated to the thesis.

## Cover Contract

Place one strong silhouette or threshold against a large atmospheric field. Reserve deterministic title space and ensure the metaphor is identifiable before any caption is read.

## Concept Contract

Use one object-state or before/after atmospheric transition to deepen a section's thesis. Do not convert prose into a collage of symbols.

## Deterministic Diagram Contract

This profile supports only simplified metaphor maps, not dense system diagrams. Map environment to `semantic_color_roles.environment`, subject/silhouette to `subject`, the single thesis cue to `thesis_accent`, distance layers to `distance`, titles to `typography.label_treatment`, and margins to the 32 px grid. Avoid node-box grammar; use large fields and one path/threshold.

## Imagegen Prompt Contract

State the thesis in one sentence, name one exact metaphor and subject, define foreground/midground/background, controlled dramatic light, atmospheric material, limited cool environment plus one warm accent, title-safe negative space, target aspect ratio, and text-free output. References anchor silhouette, light ratio, atmosphere, and restraint. Negatives: multiple symbols, faces by default, infographic modules, spectacle, labels, logos, and unrelated scenery.

## Reference Use Contract

Use the cover reference for silhouette and title-safe field, concept reference for metaphor specificity and atmosphere, and diagram reference for limited palette/spacing when a simplified map is required. Do not copy a reference metaphor if it changes the article's claim.

## Validation Rubric

Pass only if a reviewer can name the one metaphor and connect it to the thesis, one subject dominates, one accent guides the eye, title space is clean, atmosphere has depth without spectacle, and no infographic or default-face drift appears.
