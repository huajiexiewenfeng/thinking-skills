# Blueprint Linework

## Identity

- `profile_id`: `blueprint-linework`
- Protocol version: `2`
- 中文名: 蓝图线稿
- Promise: explain technical boundaries through disciplined orthographic construction and sparse blueprint annotation.

## Use When

Use for architecture boundaries, component anatomy, cutaways, infrastructure explanation, and exact visuals that must bridge concept and diagram.

## Do Not Use When

Do not use for atmospheric metaphor, spatial isometric ecosystems, high-energy launch art, or any subject that cannot be represented without invented engineering detail.

## Mode Boundary

The nearest neighbor is Isometric Infrastructure. Blueprint Linework is orthographic and line-led with almost no volume; Style 5 uses a fixed 30-degree isometric camera, matte solids, spatial scale, and directional shadows.

## Required Visual Traits

- Deep blueprint blue or warm drafting ground, uniform 2 px pale construction lines, sparse cyan highlights, and measured spacing.
- Orthographic elevation, plan, section, or cutaway; boundaries and layers are explicit and flat.
- Annotation zones are restrained and deterministic; generated backgrounds contain no invented dimensions or labels.

## Allowed Variation

Either blue or warm drafting-paper inversion is allowed. One cutaway, exploded alignment, or ghosted construction layer may clarify the real architecture.

## Forbidden Traits

No fake measurements, pseudo-engineering labels, isometric camera, photorealistic materials, dense crosshatching, dramatic shadows, or perspective distortion.

## Cover Contract

Use one large orthographic construction or cutaway as the thesis, leave a clean deterministic title area, and use cyan only to identify the article's key boundary or transition.

## Concept Contract

Explain one boundary, layer, or component relationship through a main outline plus one construction or cutaway layer. Avoid ornamental measurements and engineering theater.

## Deterministic Diagram Contract

Map ground to `surface.background`, final structure to `primary_structure`, construction/ghost lines to `construction_line`, the thesis boundary to `highlight`, secondary boundaries to `boundary`, captions to `geometry.label_style`, and layout to the 20 px drafting grid. Lines are uniform 2 px; arrows are orthogonal and unglowed.

## Imagegen Prompt Contract

Specify objective, real component or boundary, orthographic/cutaway composition, disciplined thin construction lines, blueprint or drafting surface, sparse cyan semantic highlight, target aspect ratio, and no text. References anchor projection, line density, and annotation restraint. Negatives: dimensions, labels, photorealism, isometric view, dramatic perspective, dense hatching, and decorative machinery.

## Reference Use Contract

Use the cover reference for macro construction, concept reference for cutaway abstraction, and diagram reference for exact line weights, grid, boundary colors, and typography zones. Never infer dimensions or connections from visual style alone.

## Validation Rubric

Pass only if projection is orthographic, line weight is disciplined, cyan marks one meaningful feature, no unsupported annotation appears, the drawing remains legible at article width, and no isometric or photorealistic drift is present.
