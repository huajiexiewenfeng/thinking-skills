# Polished Tech Explainer

## Identity

- `profile_id`: `blueprint-linework`
- Protocol version: `2`
- 中文名: 质感科技图解
- Promise: turn validated technical content into a polished white-blue technology product visual for covers and concepts, then a rich but precise deterministic infographic for diagrams.

## Use When

Use for AI systems, developer tools, product architecture, workflow automation, service relationships, platform capabilities, and technical processes that should look clean, credible, modern, and presentation-ready.

## Do Not Use When

Do not use for dark futuristic launch art, atmospheric cinematic metaphor, warm handmade teaching sketches, purely green editorial minimalism, or large isometric infrastructure landscapes.

## Mode Boundary

The nearest neighbors are Technical Editorial Minimal, White-Green Editorial Minimal, and Isometric Infrastructure. Style 4 uses a cool white grid, cobalt-blue system logic, navy hierarchy, polished glass-acrylic devices for covers, and flat enterprise infographics for strict diagrams. Style 1 is warmer and sparser, Style 2 is quieter and green-led, and Style 5 is spatial isometric infrastructure.

## Required Visual Traits

- Fully opaque cool white technology canvas with a faint blue drafting grid and deliberate open space.
- Deep navy information hierarchy and cobalt-blue system logic; cyan supports transitions.
- Cover and concept roles may use polished glass-acrylic panels, frosted glass capsules, white device frames, restrained blue edge light, and soft spatial shadows.
- Diagram role is a flat infographic: large rounded white containers, grid-aligned modules, cobalt paths, pale blue boundaries, subtle card shadows, blank title/subtitle zones, blank step-index discs, blank caption rails, group bands, an explanatory strip, and deterministic text.
- Teal, amber, red, and purple are semantic state colors, never decorative accents.
- Finished output includes deterministic readable Chinese or English copy; image generation remains text-free and supplies only the visual background.

## Allowed Variation

Cover scenes may combine a dominant browser, monitor, tablet, or system panel with one or two subordinate devices, glass service objects, or process modules. Concepts may use a staged transformation, modular service stack, device-to-process relation, or a left-right capability comparison with circular icons and one blue-to-green transition arrow. Diagrams may use horizontal stages, vertical lanes, or grouped regions when required by the frozen graph.

## Forbidden Traits

No dark cyanotype, black dashboard, neon cyberpunk, laser grid, floating hologram, decorative AI brain, humanoid robot, generic stock-office scene, excessive glass blur, meaningless UI, giant empty browser, chrome overload, candy-plastic toy rendering, generated labels, or unsupported topology.

## Cover Contract

Build one content-rich hybrid 2.5D technology scene with a dominant system panel and one or two subordinate technology objects. Use polished glass-acrylic depth, white satin device frames, cobalt logic, navy hierarchy, and soft spatial shadows. Generate a text-free background, then apply a deterministic headline, optional cobalt keyword emphasis, compact English subtitle, and exact annotations.

## Concept Contract

Explain one confirmed visual thesis through a polished modular technology explainer. Use one dominant focal module or transformation, two to five purposeful supporting modules, clear cobalt connection logic, selective frosted-glass depth, and generous spacing. A comparison may use two validated system panels and one transition arrow. Add exact title, panel, node, and explanatory copy deterministically. Reject generic dashboard walls, floating icon collages, meaningless app mockups, and decorative technology.

## Deterministic Diagram Contract

Freeze the graph before rendering. Map the canvas to `surface.background`, grid to `surface.grid`, normal paths to `primary`, secondary copy to `neutral`, success to `success`, human judgment or warning to `attention`, failure to `failure`, and exact wording to deterministic typography zones. Enrich the flat infographic with blank eyebrow/title/subtitle zones, blank step-index discs, blank caption rails, group bands, subtle decorative corner geometry, and one blank explanatory strip. These are information-design layers only: they must never add nodes, edges, states, or relationships. Do not use image generation for final labels.

## Imagegen Prompt Contract

Specify the confirmed visual thesis, dominant system panel or focal module, necessary supporting objects, connection logic, cool white grid, cobalt-blue palette, glass-acrylic or frosted-glass material, soft spatial shadow, target aspect ratio, occupancy, title-safe behavior, and no generated text. State which semantic colors are supported. References anchor surface, material, depth, modular hierarchy, spacing, and finish only; they never authorize copied devices, topology, labels, or story.

## Reference Use Contract

The user's first reference anchors cover material, white-blue technology atmosphere, device framing, hybrid 2.5D depth, system-panel hierarchy, and title scale. The second anchors flat-diagram containers, spacing, arrows, semantic state colors, and deterministic typography. The third anchors large navy-and-cobalt headline hierarchy, English subtitle, left-right capability comparison, circular technology icons, blue-to-green transition treatment, dense labeled workflow, and faint network ornaments. Never copy their Chinese text, devices, graph, node count, icons, or workflow.

## Validation Rubric

Pass covers only when they read as a premium technology launch visual with coherent content rather than a device collage. Pass concepts only when one relationship is understandable without generated text and material effects remain subordinate. Pass diagrams only when every visible node, edge, direction, group, state, exception, and return matches the frozen graph. All roles must remain fully opaque, legible at article width, and clearly distinct from Styles 1, 2, and 5.
