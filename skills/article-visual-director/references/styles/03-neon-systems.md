# Neon Systems

## Identity

- `profile_id`: `neon-systems`
- Protocol version: `2`
- 中文名: 霓虹系统科技
- Promise: reveal one active route through a precise dark technical system using controlled cyan and acid-green light.

## Use When

Use for agents, runtimes, observability, distributed systems, developer infrastructure, and launch narratives that need energy plus legible hierarchy.

## Do Not Use When

Do not use for calm tutorials, human-centered reflection, architectural documentation, or any topic whose content does not justify a dark modular system.

## Mode Boundary

The nearest neighbor is Blueprint Linework. Neon Systems uses layered dark modules, limited depth, and luminous signal paths; Blueprint Linework is orthographic, thin, flat, and construction-led with no glow.

## Required Visual Traits

- Navy foundation, precise modular geometry, cyan signal paths, and acid-green only for the active or successful route.
- Controlled 3 px emission, one focal path, layered-dark depth, high contrast, and a central subject isolated from background complexity.
- Dark modules remain readable without relying on random text or HUD decoration.

## Allowed Variation

The route may be radial, linear, or branching once. Glass-like separation is allowed only as subtle depth between real system layers; cyan and acid green remain the only luminous accents.

## Forbidden Traits

No cyberpunk city, rainbow neon, uncontrolled magenta, readable code, HUD clutter, lens-flare spectacle, or glow around every object.

## Cover Contract

Build one bright route or state transition through a restrained field of dark modules. Preserve title-safe negative space and prevent background modules from competing with the focal signal.

## Concept Contract

Show how a signal enters, changes, or propagates through two to five modules. Use cyan for normal flow and acid green for the one emphasized route or result.

## Deterministic Diagram Contract

Map background to `surface.background`, inactive modules to `base_module`, normal paths to `signal_path`, the single focal path to `active_route`, labels to `label`, corners/captions to `geometry`, and layout to the 24 px grid. Limit glow to 6 px around active strokes; arrows and nodes remain geometrically precise.

## Imagegen Prompt Contract

Specify objective, concrete system subject, one active route, modular dark composition, navy matte/glass layers, cyan normal signals, acid-green focus, cinematic but controlled depth, target aspect ratio, and text-free output. References anchor path hierarchy, module scale, and glow restraint. Negatives: city, people, HUD, code, magenta, rainbow, excessive bloom, and unreadable micro-detail.

## Reference Use Contract

Use the cover reference for focal-route contrast, concept reference for module abstraction, and diagram reference for exact cyan/green semantics, grid, edges, and emission limits. Do not inherit decorative background elements.

## Validation Rubric

Pass only if one route dominates, all other light is subordinate, the dark foundation remains legible, cyan and acid green have distinct semantic roles, no cyberpunk/HUD cliché appears, and deterministic assets share the same modular grammar.
