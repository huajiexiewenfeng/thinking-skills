# Technical Editorial Minimal

## Identity

- `profile_id`: `technical-editorial-minimal`
- Protocol version: `2`
- 中文名: 技术编辑简约
- Promise: reduce a technical thesis to one crisp editorial hierarchy with quiet geometry and deliberate negative space.

## Use When

Use for broad technical audiences, explanatory articles, dense tutorials, and topics whose prose or diagrams already carry substantial detail.

## Do Not Use When

Do not use when the article needs atmospheric emotion, hand-drawn warmth, spatial infrastructure, or a dark high-energy launch tone.

## Mode Boundary

The nearest neighbor is White-Green Editorial Minimal. This mode is ink-blue and neutral with one warm exception; Style 2 is green-led, wider, and more active across the canvas. If emerald hierarchy dominates, select Style 2.

## Required Visual Traits

- Off-white matte surface, ink-blue structure, one blue signal, and at most one warm semantic exception.
- Flat grid, crisp 3 px edges, 8 px corners, low-to-medium density, and large unoccupied breathing zones.
- One focal metaphor or comparison; supporting shapes remain subordinate and label-free when generated.

## Allowed Variation

The canvas may invert to deep ink for a cover, and the focal object may be abstract or literal. Keep the same grid, flat depth, restrained palette, and modern sans hierarchy.

## Forbidden Traits

No hand-drawn strokes, dashboard chrome, glossy 3D modules, decorative circuitry, tiny UI text, stock-photo people, or multiple competing accents.

## Cover Contract

Build one dominant geometric thesis across 60–80% of the wide canvas, preserve a deterministic title-safe zone, and use the warm accent only for the article's exception, pressure, or human factor.

## Concept Contract

Express one causal relationship with two to four crisp objects. Use space as grouping; avoid card piles and decorative frames.

## Deterministic Diagram Contract

Map paper to `surface.background`, boundaries and text to `line.primary`, primary paths to `semantic_color_roles.primary_signal`, exceptions to `human_or_exception`, neutral nodes to `neutral`, captions to `geometry.label_style`, and all placement to the 24 px spacing grid. Arrows are straight or single-elbow, 3 px, and never glowing.

## Imagegen Prompt Contract

State the article objective and one subject/metaphor; require a flat editorial grid, crisp matte geometry, restrained ink-blue/blue/warm semantic roles, generous negative space, and the target aspect ratio. Attach approved references and preserve their hierarchy, edge behavior, density, and surface. Request a text-free background. Negatives: hand drawing, dashboard, 3D, decorative circuitry, crowd, readable labels, and extra accent colors.

## Reference Use Contract

Use the approved cover to anchor wide hierarchy, the concept image to anchor abstraction level, and the deterministic diagram to anchor exact palette, line, spacing, and typography behavior. Never copy incidental objects from a reference when they do not serve the new thesis.

## Validation Rubric

Pass only if the thesis is readable at thumbnail size, the palette uses no more than one cool and one warm accent, edges are crisp and flat, the layout is not a dashboard, deterministic and generated assets look related, and all forbidden traits are absent.
