# White-Green Editorial Minimal

## Identity

- `profile_id`: `white-green-editorial-minimal`
- Protocol version: `2`
- 中文名: 白底绿色编辑简约
- Promise: create bright, rational technology editorials whose emerald hierarchy actively spans a wide canvas.

## Use When

Use for WeChat technology commentary, AI industry analysis, balanced comparisons, and premium bright covers that should feel energetic without becoming cyberpunk.

## Do Not Use When

Do not use for sustainability branding, botanical themes, dark launch visuals, soft watercolor teaching, or pages where green is merely the publication theme rather than the selected visual profile.

## Mode Boundary

The nearest neighbor is Technical Editorial Minimal. Style 2 must be visibly green-led and horizontally active; Style 1 is ink-blue, quieter, and more neutral. A pale green tint alone does not qualify as Style 2.

## Required Visual Traits

- White or pale mint surface with forest/emerald hierarchy and sufficient contrast.
- Flat matte geometry, crisp 3 px edges, 12 px corners, one semantic nucleus, and meaningful structures extending into both outer bands.
- Intentional foreground occupies roughly 70–90% of a 2.35:1 cover while the central square crop preserves the primary thesis.

## Allowed Variation

Small cyan-green accents, restrained soft shadows, and asymmetrical left/right forces are allowed. The work must remain brand-neutral, bright, and flat.

## Forbidden Traits

No leaves, sustainability symbolism, pale low-contrast card piles, glassmorphism, dense circuitry, empty outer bands, excessive glow, or a composition shrunk entirely into the central square.

## Cover Contract

Use one central semantic nucleus with left and right forces that continue beyond the square crop. Reserve a deterministic title zone without emptying the wide frame; outer content must carry meaning rather than decoration.

## Concept Contract

Show one tension, transition, or three-part system using emerald hierarchy and mint support. Group through alignment and active space, not stacks of generic cards.

## Deterministic Diagram Contract

Map canvas to `surface.background`, major boundaries/text to `semantic_color_roles.hierarchy`, active nodes and arrows to `active_system`, support areas to `support`, secondary paths to `secondary_signal`, captions to `geometry.label_style`, and spacing to the 24 px grid. Keep 3 px crisp lines and matte-flat depth.

## Imagegen Prompt Contract

State objective, central subject, left/right semantic forces, wide composition, clean matte material, emerald hierarchy, pale mint support, active 70–90% occupancy, target aspect ratio, and text-free output. With references, preserve wide occupancy, green contrast, nucleus scale, and flat edge behavior. Negatives: leaves, eco imagery, card pile, glass, glow, UI chrome, empty side bands, and embedded text.

## Reference Use Contract

The approved cover anchors crop behavior and wide occupancy; the concept anchors green semantic hierarchy; the diagram anchors exact tokens, line weight, captions, and spacing. Publication green must never override these approved profile roles.

## Validation Rubric

Pass only if the canvas is bright, green hierarchy is unmistakable, side bands contain meaningful support, the nucleus survives the square crop, pale elements retain contrast, the result is flat/matte rather than glassy, and no sustainability cliché appears.
